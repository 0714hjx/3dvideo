# Material-study assets

- `earth.jpg`, `wood.jpg`, `clay.jpg`, `stone.jpg`: AI-generated material atlas, split into four surface maps for the original 3D scene. These are synthesized materials, not measured scans of a historical building.
- `sans.otf`: subset of Noto Sans CJK SC. Font license: `../changan/FONT-LICENSE.txt`.

The user-supplied reference video is used to analyze presentation style only. Its frames, audio and architectural model are not redistributed with this project. The reference shows Beijing's Temple of Heaven; this study does not copy its Ming/Qing circular roof or decorative program into Tang Chang'an.
